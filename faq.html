FAQ

Straight answers.

Practices, agreements, brands, metro range, ducts. The questions owners actually ask.

Do you do residential?

Yes — luxury New York residential. Penthouses, townhouses, brownstones, high-floor co-ops and condos. White-glove: freight, floor protection, building staff, quiet hours. We do not do suburban split-systems, coupon residential, or a single PTAC. The threshold is seriousness of architecture and equipment, not a slogan. See Residences.

What kind of home is in range?

Where the architecture and the equipment are both serious: multi-zone VRF or hydronic, historic fabric, board rules, millwork, specialty rooms. A full-floor apartment that must stay livable. A landmarked townhouse. Not a starter condo with through-wall units.

What is a service agreement, here?

Commercial: Preventive, Full labor, or Full coverage — time, quality, and cost named in English, with a dedicated account team, parts in LIC, after-hours. Residential: named technicians who know the apartment and 24/7 priority. Refrigeration: after-close care and a desk that treats product-loss as a clock. None of these is a PDF with a gold stamp.

Refrigeration versus HVAC — do I need two companies?

Usually no, if the roof, the service, and the night are shared. Walk-ins, racks, ice, hoods, and dining HVAC belong in one set. We wrote an essay. A standalone office tower with no food can stay in commercial. A standalone box can stay in refrigeration. A kitchen on a mixed roof should not be split.

Which brands do you work on?

Any brand a New York building actually has. Daily: Carrier, Daikin, LG, Mitsubishi, Samsung, Trane, York, AAON, Multistack, and the applied and controls names on the commercial index. We are not a closed dealer page. Obscure tags still get opened.

How far do you go?

Five boroughs, then Long Island, Westchester, southern Connecticut, northern New Jersey. Manhattan emergency target is ninety minutes. Metro travel is written into the agreement. We do not pretend a van in Hudson Square is already in Greenwich.

Do you clean ducts?

When it is worth it: construction dust, a real contamination event, kitchen grease on its own cycle, healthcare when infection control asks. When it is not: a well-filtered office with intact lining and no event. We will not destroy lining for a photograph, and we will not sell a coupon. See duct cleaning.

How do you price?

Design is a fee. Installation is a bid or a negotiated GMP, depending on how complete the set is. Maintenance is an annual agreement with a defined asset list. Emergency without a contract is time-and-material at published rates. We do not bid design-build from a napkin and pretend the number will hold.

Are you union?

We are union-aware NYC commercial. Union sites are staffed as union sites. PLA jobs are PLA jobs. Residential and some kitchens are a different labor conversation — we will not discover it at the gate. Talk to us early if the building has a history.

Local Law 97 — can you guarantee compliance?

We can design a pathway and engineer the plant to it. Occupancy, operations, and future tenants remain yours. We will not sell a guarantee that ignores how the building is run. See the journal essay. Residences are a different carbon conversation; we still tell the truth about steam and electric service.

Do you do LL87 audits and LL88 lighting?

LL87 yes, and we prefer to then do the work the audit named. LL88 lighting when it shares a ceiling, a panel, or a filing with our mechanical work. We are not a lighting-only shop.

Shutdown windows?

Written into the schedule, usually overnight or weekend. Occupied hospitals, hotels, data halls, homes, and kitchens get phasing and temporary plant or cold. A shutdown that “should only take a few hours” is a sentence we rewrite.

Response times?

Emergency Manhattan target: 90 minutes. Maintenance agreement times are site-specific and written. Studio calls during business hours: a principal or project engineer the same day if the job is active.

New construction vs retrofit?

Both. Retrofit is most of New York. New construction is when the GC needs a mechanical partner who already knows a Manhattan dock — or a kitchen opening. We are not a national production builder.

Will you bid against three other contractors from an incomplete set?

Sometimes, with qualifications. Often we will offer to complete the set first. A cheap number on a bad drawing is how plants fail in year two.

Do we need a PE?

In New York, often. Alterations, new plants, anything the DOB will treat as design. We stamp what we control. We will not stamp a set we did not author.

Can you maintain a plant you did not install?

Yes, after a condition survey. We will not inherit fiction. Capital surprises get named in month one, not month eleven. Same for a walk-in or a penthouse VRF.

Incentives and rebates?

NYSERDA and Con Edison programs when they are real for the measure. We package them. We do not pad a job to chase a rebate that will not land.

Insurance and COI?

Yes. Certificates in the format loading docks and management offices already expect, before anyone asks. Limits discussed in the contract, not at the door.

Who owns the BAS credentials?

You do. We keep a backup under contract. We do not hold a building hostage to a login.

How do we start?

The proposal form, or the studio line. Emergency: the other number. A walk in the plant, the apartment, or the kitchen is the usual second step.
