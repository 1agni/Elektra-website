Retrofit & carbon

Local Law 97 is a limit, not a slogan. We right-size plants, recover heat, electrify when the service allows, and file a pathway that an owner can fund.

What it is

Retrofit is the majority of New York’s mechanical future. The buildings exist. The plants are the age they are. The law has a number on it. We treat carbon as a design constraint — like acoustics, like floor-to-floor, like a landmark façade. The work is heat recovery, plant right-sizing, envelope-aware loads, controls that stop heating and cooling the same atrium, and electrification where Con Edison and the switchgear will actually accept it.

Local Law 87 energy audits are a document we use, not a PDF we produce and forget. Local Law 88 lighting is adjacent; we coordinate when it shares a ceiling or a panel. The point is a pathway to 2030 and 2035 that an asset manager can put in a model, not a press release.

When a building needs it

When the 2024 LL97 penalty is a rounding error and the 2030 penalty is not. When steam is leaving the building and the owner wants to know what replaces it. When a plant is twice the tons the occupancy needs. When the BAS is a timeclock with a logo.

How we work

Measure the building you have. Interval data, a plant trend, a walk. Then options: optimize first, recover heat second, replace third, electrify when the service and the budget agree. We will not sell a heat pump that the feeder cannot carry. We will not ignore a tower that is the real problem.

The sage-colored part of our practice is this work — energy as material. We file, we model, we phase so the building stays open. Incentives (NYSERDA, Con Edison) are pursued when they are real, not when they are a brochure.

What you get

  • LL97 pathway with numbers, not adjectives
  • Plant right-sizing and heat-recovery schemes
  • Electrification feasibility including service and steam exit
  • Phased construction in occupied buildings
  • Incentive packaging where it exists
  • A capital calendar the board can vote on

New York

LL97, LL87, LL88. DOB filings for alterations. Steam-to-hydronic conversions that respect Con Edison and the pipe in the street. Landmarked roofs. Neighbors who will hear a new tower. We design for all of it. Sage is the color we use when we talk about this — once, on purpose.

Questions

Will you guarantee LL97 compliance?

We will design a pathway and engineer the plant to it. Occupancy and operations remain the owner’s. We do not sell magic.

Heat pumps in a tower?

Sometimes. The service, the envelope, and the peak are the questions. We answer them before we specify.

Do you do LL87 audits?

Yes, and we prefer to be the ones who then do the work the audit named.
