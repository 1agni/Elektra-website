Work order closeout

A visit that disappears into a ticket number is not a visit. Every emergency and every repair closes in writing.

  • Written summary: what failed, what we did, what is still open
  • Photographs of the condition and of the work, not a vanity shot of a van
  • Parts used, parts on order, lead time named honestly
  • Follow-up: who owns the next action, and by when
  • A verbal to the chief, the night porter, or the manager before we leave the dock

NYC operational reality

The city is not a footnote. Vendor onboarding, the freight elevator, and the law are the job. We do not discover them on day two.

COI & onboardingCertificate on file before the first visit. Additional insured language as the building writes it. Vendor portals, badging, site-specific orientations — we complete them. We will not send a tech to wait in the lobby for a PDF.
Building accessFreight notes live with the dispatcher, not in someone’s head. Loading hours, pad, roof hatch, after-hours escort, house rules. Union sites stay union sites.
Local 638We are aware. Steamfitter work is staffed as steamfitter work. We do not pretend a service tech is a fit-up crew, and we do not pick a fight with the hall on your loading dock.
DOB / FDNYWhen the work requires a filing, we file. Permits, progress inspections, FDNY where fuel, smoke, or a place of assembly is in scope. PE stamp on design work. We sit in the meeting.
Shutdowns & crane daysWindows the city and the building actually allow. After-hours and weekend work are the default on occupied towers, not an upsell. A crane day is scheduled as a crane day — DOT, the roof, the GC, the weather.
Local Law 97 / 87 / 88An operations problem, not a marketing banner. Reporting, EUI, the plant versus the lighting. LL87 audits that become work. LL88 where it touches our scope. A PE who will sign the pathway. Limits first, then a plant that meets them.
RefrigerantEPA 608 on the bench. Leak logs that exist. NYC rules at the level a chief can hand to an inspector — charge, recovery, the record — without a seminar.
