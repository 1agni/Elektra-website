Quiet is a hospital system

Pressure relationships get signed. Noise should. A mechanical floor is a clinical neighbor.

Ask a hospital facilities director what failed last summer and you will hear about a chiller, a valve, a BAS graphic that lied. Ask a nurse on a night shift what failed and you may hear about a rumble in the ceiling over a sleep study, or a fan cycling in an ICU that has otherwise been designed to within an inch of its life. Infection control has a signature block on our drawings. Acoustics often have a note. The note is not enough.

A hospital is a 24-hour instrument. Air moves because pressure must move: from OR to corridor, from corridor to isolation, never the other way unless the room is written that way. That air is a system. The sound of that air is also a system. We treat it as one at Elektra because we have watched projects that passed every pressure test and still failed the floor below the plant.

The mechanical floor is a neighbor

In New York, the mechanical room is rarely a shed at the end of a driveway. It is a floor. Under it there might be imaging, or sleep, or a NICU that was not on the original stacking plan. A centrifugal machine with bad isolation is a clinical event. Vibration through structure does not care that your specification said “inertia base.” If the springs are the wrong deflection, if the floor was never floating, if the duct is tied to the deck, the room below will know.

We specify 2-inch deflection where the program needs it, a floating floor when the stack is unkind, silencers on discharges that face patient rooms, and a construction protocol that puts noisy trades at night only after nursing has the notice. This is not courtesy. It is how you avoid a variance from a department that has already had a week.

Redundancy without a second noise

N+1 is a hospital sentence. It is also how you double a sound source if you are careless. Two machines that idle badly are worse than one that runs well. Magnetic-bearing compressors have been our preference on recent wings not because they are fashionable, but because oil management and vibration are failure modes we would rather not explain to a medical director. The spare must be exercised. A standby that has not run in years is not N+1. It is a story.

Temporary cooling during construction is part of the acoustic problem. A rental skid on a dock roof can be a good neighbor or a bad one. We screen, we isolate, we tell the block. FDNY cares about the fuel. The sleep unit cares about the fan. Both are right.

Air that does not announce itself

OR dual-path systems exist so that recirculation can be clean and outdoor air can be separate. They also exist so that you are not throwing 20 air changes of untreated noise at a table. Terminal HEPA, lined duct, velocities that a room can live with. Isolation rooms need offsets that are monitored; the monitor should not beep as a personality. Alarms that reach nursing must be the ones that matter. A BAS that cries all night is a BAS that will be muted, and then you have no system at all.

We commission pressure with infection control in the room. We commission sound with a meter, not with an opinion, and we do it at night because that is when the floor is a hospital and not a construction site. If the number is wrong, we do not write a variance for ourselves. We fix the hanger.

Occupied construction is the job

The wing stays open. That sentence is the design. Phasing, anterooms, ICRA, negative pressure on the work, a walk every morning with facilities. After-hours for demolition. A posted quiet protocol. Union labor that has done healthcare, not just towers. DOB and DOHMH in parallel. If your schedule assumes a dark floor, you are not in a hospital.

Lenox Hill, as we built it, kept cooling at 100% through the work. That is the only acceptable headline. The sleep-study staff reporting that the mechanical floor was “finally gone” was the acoustic headline. We put them in the same closeout because they are the same job.

What quiet costs

It costs springs, mass, silencers, night labor, a rental plant, and the refusal to run a cheaper machine that will be heard. It is less than a diverted OR day. It is less than a year of reviews that mention the HVAC, if you are a hospital that still thinks that way. Mostly it is a decision to treat the person in the bed as the client. The drawing set can bear that. It should.
