Refrigeration · 03

After close. Before the inspector.

NYC food safety is a calendar. Coils, leaks, door gaskets, temperatures that are logged. We work when the line is dark.

What it is

Condensers that have not seen a coil brush since the last ownership. Walk-in evaporators iced because someone keeps propping the door. A leak rate that has become a habit. Ice machines on a cleaning cycle that matches the health code, not a memory. Temperature logs that a manager can actually show. We write a program from the equipment list in the kitchen, not from a generic food-service binder.

New York

DOHMH does not care about our brochure. They care about the box. Grease and refrigeration share a room; we coordinate with hood cleaning so we are not washing coils the night after a grease scrape, or the reverse. After close is the default. Grocery overnight is a different clock — we write it into the SLA. Product stays in the box unless we have a plan.

What you get

  • Scheduled coil, gasket, and leak work
  • Ice machine hygiene on a real interval
  • A log the manager can hand an inspector
  • Priority at the emergency desk
  • A capital note when a box is finished
