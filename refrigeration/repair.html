Refrigeration · 01

Hold the box.

A warm cooler is a clock. Diagnose, recover, repair. Say when the coil is finished. Do not invent a season of optimism over someone else’s inventory.

What it is

Walk-ins that ice on one wall. Reach-ins that cannot pull down after a rush. Racks with a circuit out. Ice machines that taste like the last service. Prep tables that sweat onto food. We recover refrigerant as the law requires, find leaks as a method, and repair what is honest. We work on the brands a New York kitchen actually bought — not a closed list.

How we work

After close when we can; during service when product is at risk. We do not block a line without the chef knowing. Temporary product movement is a plan, not a scramble. If the repair is a compressor and the box is full, we talk about dry ice, a sister walk-in, or a truck — before we pull the machine apart.
