Refrigeration · 04

Boxes, racks, new kitchens.

A walk-in is a room with a climate and a drain. A rack is a plant. We install them with the HVAC and the hood, not after the ceiling is closed.

What it is

New walk-ins, replacements, parallel racks for grocery and commissary, remote condensers, the electrical and the dunnage, the drain that will not freeze, the heat that will not dump into a dining room. We coordinate with the GC, the hood shop if it is not us, and the landlord’s engineer who has a roof already spoken for.

How we work

Measure the kitchen that exists. Load the menu, not the brochure. Draw the roof with HVAC so two trades are not fighting for the last square of structure. File when the DOB treats it as an alteration. Commission to temperature, not to a handshake. A restaurant opening date is a real constraint; we will say if the equipment lead time does not match it.
