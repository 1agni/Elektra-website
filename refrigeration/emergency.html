Refrigeration · 02

Dinner rush is a clock.

Product loss. A DOH inspector in the morning. A walk-in that will not pull down. Call the desk. This is not a ticket for Tuesday.

What counts

A cooler or freezer leaving its band with product inside. A rack down on a Saturday. An ice machine dead for a full house. A leak you can smell in a dining room. A hood that has stopped in service — that is kitchen, same desk. If it is a dirty condenser and you have been meaning to call, that is maintenance.

What to have ready

  • Address, borough, and how we get to the kitchen after hours
  • What is in the box, roughly, and how long it has been warm
  • Whether you can move product, and to where
  • Whether DOH or a delivery is in the morning

The first hour

Dispatch with an ETA. Stabilize: door discipline, temporary cooling if we have it, a decision about product. Repair if it is honest tonight. If it is not, we say so while there is still a decision to make. Contracted kitchens are first. We still take others when the bench allows.

The number is the same desk as plant emergencies: (212) 555-0197. Tell them it is refrigeration. How the desk is staffed.
