Refrigeration · 05

The line that makes heat.

Ranges, ovens, steamers, hoods, makeup. Cold keeps product. Hot cooks it. The roof receives both. One company, so the arguments happen on paper.

Hot side

Cooking equipment is a mechanical load: gas, electric, steam, the exhaust that must capture, the makeup that must arrive without blowing out a pilot or a guest. We specify, install, and service the line when it is part of a kitchen we already hold — and we will take a hood-only job when the capture is the problem. We are not a restaurant-supply showroom. We are the studio that already knows what the condenser on the roof is doing.

Hoods

Type I and Type II, listed assemblies, makeup, demand control when it is real, fire suppression coordinated with the fire alarm and FDNY. Grease duct wrapping, risers, the neighbor, the landmark roof. Cleaning cycles that match the code and the menu. A hood that was sized for a different line will not be flattered by a new range; we say so.

Why one company

Makeup air is HVAC. Exhaust is kitchen. Heat rejection is refrigeration. Split them and the dining room is cold while the pass is impossible. We keep the three in one set of drawings and one account team. The essay.
