Sectors · 07

Food service

Kitchens are plants with menus. Heat, grease, cold, and guests share a roof.

Who it is for

Independent restaurants, groups, hotel food and beverage, catering commissaries, institutional kitchens. Culinary directors, GCs who have already poured the floor, landlords who have a remaining rectangle of roof.

Typical systems

Walk-ins and racks. Type I and II hoods, makeup, demand control when it is real. Kitchen HVAC that assumes the line is on. Dining HVAC that does not. Heat reclaim when warewash will run anyway. Grease duct as a rated assembly, not a leftover.

Constraints

DOHMH. FDNY. Opening dates that are catering contracts. A roof already spoken for. Makeup that can starve a hood or freeze a guest. Overnight work because lunch is not a shutdown.

Our point of view

We will not split refrigeration, HVAC, and the hood into three vendors and call that coordination. See the commissary. See the kitchen practice. See the essay on one company.
