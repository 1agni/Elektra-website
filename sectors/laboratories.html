Sectors · 08

Laboratories

A lab is not an office with more exhaust. Air, pressure, and the alarm that reaches the right person.

Who it is for

Research floors, teaching labs, QC labs in industry, the occasional clinical lab that is not a hospital. Facilities, EH&S, and the PI who will notice a pressure excursion before the BAS does.

Typical systems

Once-through or high-air-change systems. Manifolded exhaust where it is allowed. Pressurization that is a sequence, not a sticker. Redundant chilled water or DX for equipment rooms. Snorkels, hoods, and the makeup that keeps them from stealing a corridor. BAS alarms that reach EH&S.

Constraints

Occupied research. Animals, if they exist, are a 24/7 constraint. Fume hoods that cannot go down for a convenient afternoon. Campus standards. DOB and the fire department when the exhaust is the architecture.

Our point of view

We treat the lab as a process. Temporary air before we touch a riser. Commissioning that EH&S attends. Healthcare is a sibling sector when the lab is clinical; data is a sibling when the room is a freezer farm.
