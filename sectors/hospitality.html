Sectors · 03

Hospitality

A guest does not care about your tons. They care about sleep, a shower that is hot, and a restaurant that does not smell like the corridor.

Who it is for

Hotels, clubs, and the restaurants attached to them. Owners and operators who read reviews as operations data. Brands that have a noise standard whether they wrote it down or not. Theaters and health clubs belong here when the occupancy is entertainment or wellness: show hours, acoustics, pools, humidity. Hotel kitchens share the refrigeration practice.

Typical systems

Guest-room VRF or four-pipe fan coils, corridor DOAS, kitchen grease exhaust and makeup, laundry, pools, rooftop heat pumps. Occupancy from the lock system. A BAS that the night engineer can use at 2am without a vendor.

Constraints

NC targets in rooms. Neighbors and noise ordinances. Grease risers and FDNY. Roofs that are amenities. Full occupancy during the work. A kitchen that cannot close on a Saturday. Reviews.

Our point of view

Hospitality HVAC is hospitality. If the equipment is audible, we failed. If the roof is ugly, we failed the architect. EDITION Times Square is the case: PTACs out, NC-25 in, kitchen stack that does not announce the building.
