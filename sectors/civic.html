Sectors · 06

Civic

Rooms the city owns. Landmark fabric, public hours, a procurement that is slow, a standard that should not be.

Who it is for

Libraries, museums, houses of faith, agencies, campuses that sit in the civic realm even when they are private. Collections climate for museums; quiet and landmark fabric for faith buildings. Public clients and the construction managers who serve them. Schools have their own page.

Typical systems

Hidden DOAS, original radiation kept as architecture, occupancy-based ventilation, filtration for crowds and for wildfire weeks, BAS the City can own. Steam houses that will not vanish this year.

Constraints

LPC. Public hours. Procurement. Union. Occupancy that swings from empty to a thousand. Budgets that are real. A hall that must still sound like a hall.

Our point of view

The architecture is the client. We hide the air. We measure after. Brooklyn Public Library Central is the case: occupancy reset, millwork cabinets, no duct in the great hall.
