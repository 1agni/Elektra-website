Education

Classrooms, lecture halls, dorms, and the kitchen that serves them. Public hours, union, a calendar that is the client.

Who it is for

Independent schools, universities, the occasional public capital job. Facilities directors, construction managers, boards that meet at 7am.

Typical systems

Occupancy-based ventilation, quiet terminals over teaching, kitchens and dining as food-service physics, dorms that are residences with a RA on the door, labs as labs. BAS the school can own. Steam houses that will not vanish this year.

Constraints

Bells. Quiet hours for exams. Summer work that is never as empty as promised. Landmark campuses. Parents. Procurement. Union.

Our point of view

Civic is the cousin — libraries and agencies. Education is a calendar. We hide the air, we do not shut down a midterm, and we treat the kitchen as a kitchen. Related civic work at the library; related food work at the commissary.
