Sectors · 02

Healthcare

A hospital is a process. Air, pressure, redundancy, noise, and time. The climate system is clinical equipment.

Who it is for

Hospitals, ambulatory wings, procedure floors, and the occasional research clinic. Facilities, infection control, and capital planning in the same meeting — or the job will fail later.

Typical systems

N+1 and N+2 chilled water. Dual-path OR air. HEPA terminals. Isolation rooms with monitored offsets. Dedicated exhaust. Steam to sterile processing. BAS with critical alarms that reach nursing, not just engineering. Emergency power that actually includes the fans you think it includes.

Constraints

The building does not close. Infection control signs drawings. Acoustics over sleep units and NICUs. FGI and ASHRAE 170. DOHMH. Redundancy that is a machine, a pipe, and a sequence — not a sticker. Construction dust is a clinical event.

Our point of view

Quiet is a hospital system. We wrote an essay about that. The work is temporary cooling before demolition, isolation that is physical, and a commissioning that infection control attends. See Lenox Hill.
