Sectors · 04

Data centers

Watts per rack, water in the right place, a leak that is a sentence. Density is the brief.

Who it is for

Research computing, commercial colo, and the on-prem halls that enterprises still run in Manhattan and LIC. IT and facilities in one room, or we will put them there.

Typical systems

In-row, rear-door heat exchangers, CRAH, dedicated CHW plants, free cooling where the climate and the owner agree (rarely, here), leak detection, aspirating smoke, dual-cord everything that matters. Containment. Sequences that reset against IT, not against a comfort setpoint.

Constraints

Rack density that outruns last year’s drawing. Floor loading. Water over live equipment. Campus plants that are not yours. Uptime as a contract. A university calendar. Liquid cooling arriving faster than the SOP.

Our point of view

We will not air-cool 40 kW because someone is afraid of water. We will put water in a door, isolate it, detect it, and commission the fail-close. Columbia Data Hall is the work.
