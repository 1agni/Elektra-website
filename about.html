Studio

An engineer and a builder.

Maya Chen, PE, and Luis Navarro opened Elektra Mechanical in 2014 because the city’s plants were being treated as commodities and the buildings deserved better. The practice now holds commercial, residences, and refrigeration as one studio.

The studio

Maya came from a mechanical engineering practice that stamped good sets and never saw the roof. Luis came from a construction side that saw the roof and inherited sets that could not be built. They met on a Midtown plant replacement that had both problems. Elektra is the correction: design and field in one practice, Hudson Square and Long Island City, paper and truck.

We are about eighty-five people. Engineers, superintendents, technicians, estimators, BAS specialists, refrigeration mechanics, residential techs who know how to enter a finished apartment, a small studio staff that keeps the work looking like the work. Licensed. Insured. Union-aware. Master plumber. Master HVAC. PE stamps. We file with DOB. We sit with FDNY. We know what a landmark staff hearing feels like, and what a co-op board packet requires.

Three practices, one account

Commercial remains the center of gravity: towers, hospitals, hotels, data, civic, retail — repair through plant design. Residences is luxury New York only: penthouses, townhouses, high-floor co-ops, white-glove access. Refrigeration and kitchen is food: walk-ins, racks, hoods, the roof that has to take all of it. Account teams can hold a tower and a penthouse and a commissary without inventing three vendors. Parts that matter live in LIC. After-hours is a desk, not a voicemail.

The culture is calm on purpose. Short sentences in the office because the field is loud enough. We hire people who can stand in a mechanical room, a finished living room, and a live kitchen without changing register in a cheap way.

Licenses and filings

New York City licensed contractors. Master plumber. Master HVAC. Professional engineering for the design work that requires it. COIs that loading docks and management offices accept. We are not a national brand with a local answering service. The studio is at 200 Hudson Street, Suite 800. The yard is at 27-01 47th Avenue, LIC.

How we hire

A conversation, a walk through a job if it helps, references we actually call. We look for craft and judgment. Open roles live on the careers page — including refrigeration and residential service. Interns from CCNY, NYU Tandon, and the trades — paid.
