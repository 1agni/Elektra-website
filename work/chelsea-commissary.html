09 · Chelsea · Kitchen · 2024

Chelsea commissary

Location

West Chelsea production kitchen

Year

2024

Client

Restaurant group / in-house culinary

Scope

Parallel racks, walk-ins, HVAC, Type I hoods and makeup — one mechanical contract so the roof was designed once.

Systems

Two parallel compressor racks serving walk-in coolers, a freezer, and a bank of prep tables. Remote condensers on a new dunnage frame shared with kitchen exhaust fans and a dedicated makeup unit. Kitchen HVAC as a dehumidifying DOAS plus space cooling that assumes the hood is on. Demand-controlled Type I hoods over a production line, fire suppression tied to the building alarm. Heat reclaim off the racks into domestic hot water for warewash.

The problem

The group had been told, by three vendors, that refrigeration, HVAC, and the hood were three jobs. The landlord had one remaining roof rectangle. The electrical service had a number that would not accept three independent peak stories. The opening date was a catering contract, not a wish. A prior walk-in in the same shell had dumped heat into a corridor the DOHMH already disliked.

The approach

One set. Racks sized to the menu’s actual pull-down, not a catalog maximum. Condensers and exhaust fans on a shared steel frame, structurally reviewed, with a screen the architect could live with. Makeup air from a unit that dehumidifies; the dining-adjacent tasting room would not go clammy when the line was at full fire. Heat reclaim because warewash was going to run all afternoon and the racks were going to reject heat anyway.

Phasing: temporary cold boxes so R&D could continue in a corner. After-close welding. A grease duct that was wrapped and inspected before the ceiling closed. Commissioning with the culinary director present for the first full production day — temperatures, capture, and the moment the hood ramps.

The outcome

The roof has one story, not three. Summer kitchen dry-bulb holds. Walk-ins pull down after a production spike without borrowing capacity from the freezer. DHW from reclaim covers a measurable share of warewash. We hold the maintenance: refrigeration after close, HVAC on the commercial calendar, hood cleaning on the grease cycle — one account engineer.
