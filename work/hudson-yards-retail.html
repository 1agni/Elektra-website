04 · Hudson Yards · Mixed-use retail · 2020

Hudson Yards Retail

Location

20 Hudson Yards, West Side

Year

2020

Client

Retail master landlord / mixed-use

Scope

High-ventilation retail block with atrium smoke control, tenant turnover, and a public realm that cannot smell like a kitchen.

Systems

Central DOAS at 180,000 CFM with enthalpy wheels and MERV-14. Tenant exhaust risers with grease, general, and dishwasher classifications. Atrium smoke exhaust at 6 air changes, interlocked with fire alarm and pressurization of egress. CO demand-control in the below-grade garage. Base-building BAS with tenant BACnet handoff. Kitchen makeup from the landlord plant so operators cannot starve the mall of air.

The problem

A retail block fails in public. If the atrium stratifies, if a kitchen dumps odor into the court, if smoke control is a drawing that never got commissioned, the landlord owns the story. Tenant turnover is constant: a restaurant becomes a flagship becomes a restaurant again. The base building has to absorb that without redesigning the plant every lease.

FDNY had opinions about the atrium. They were correct. The original smoke model assumed a smaller fire load than the merchandising plan that actually arrived. We re-ran it.

The approach

We treated ventilation as a utility, like power. A landlord DOAS sized for the block, not for a single tenant’s opening date. Enthalpy wheels because 180,000 CFM of untreated outdoor air in August is a plant, not a gesture. Tenant guidelines: maximum exhaust, grease riser locations, makeup air that must come from us. The alternative is a mall that lists.

Smoke: exhaust fans on emergency power, make-up paths that do not fight the stairs, a sequence tested with FDNY in the space — not on paper. Garage CO at 25 ppm start, 35 ppm high-speed. After-hours work only; the public floors stayed open. BIM with the GC for every riser crossing a feature ceiling.

The outcome

Atrium dry-bulb variation on a July Saturday: 3°F floor to 40 feet. Smoke-control test accepted on the second run. Tenant odor complaints against the court: two in year one, both traced to a tenant who had bypassed makeup; both corrected. The plant has absorbed four restaurant turnovers without a base-building change order.
