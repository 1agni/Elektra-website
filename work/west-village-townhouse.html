08 · West Village · Residences · 2023

West Village townhouse

Location

West Village, landmarked row

Year

2023

Client

Private household / preservation architect

Scope

Hydronic plant, dedicated outdoor air, discreet terminal devices. LPC and a co-op-adjacent neighbor wall.

Systems

Air-to-water heat pump in the cellar on a raised housekeeping pad, with a small condensing boiler remaining as extreme-weather backup until the electrical service upgrade lands. Radiant in new floors where the structure allowed; low-temperature fan coils behind original millwork elsewhere. DOAS with ERV in a roof bulkhead that LPC would accept. No through-wall condensers. No invented cornice louvers.

The problem

Steam was leaving the block on a schedule the household did not control. Window units had been the summer, and the landmark staff had noticed. The cellar floods in a king tide. The stair is the architecture; it would not become a shaft. A neighbor’s party wall could not take vibration. The household would occupy the garden level through construction.

The approach

We measured infiltration because the envelope is the HVAC. Loads came in lower than a gut-reno rule of thumb, which let the heat pump stay in a size the electrical service could almost accept. LPC: a bulkhead no taller than the adjacent, painted with the roof, intake and discharge that do not face the garden. Cellar: equipment above a flood line, disconnects that can be killed from the stair, a drain that assumes water.

Radiant only where new floors were already in the architectural scope. Elsewhere, coils in existing millwork with returns that look like the house. Quiet hours for the party wall. A temporary split on the garden level so the household did not live in a dust story.

The outcome

Steam gone from the apartment. Summer without window units. LPC closed the application without a second hearing. The cellar plant survived a wet autumn because it was designed to. We remain for seasonal care; the same technician who commissioned it still holds the key from the super.
