01 · Hudson Yards · Commercial tower · 2023

One Manhattan West

Location

395 Ninth Avenue, Hudson Yards West

Year

2023

Client

Commercial landlord / Class A tower

Scope

Central plant modernization and a Local Law 97 pathway through 2030.

Systems

Twin 1,200-ton centrifugal chillers with a 400-ton heat-recovery machine on the same header. Induced-draft cooling towers with VFDs and basin heaters. Primary-secondary chilled water, 42/56°F design. VAV with hot-water reheat at the floors. New Niagara-based BAS with fault detection on the plant.

The problem

The 2019 plant was still running, but it was sized for a building that never fully leased as drawn. Two oversized machines, constant-speed towers, a sequence that staged like 1998. Energy use intensity sat at 58 kBtu/sf·yr. LL97’s 2030 limit for this occupancy would have put the asset into penalty without a plant conversation — not a lighting one.

Nighttime condenser water noise had already drawn a neighbor complaint from the block to the west. Any new tower work had to come in quieter, not louder. The existing electrical service could not accept a full electrified heating plant without a Con Edison upgrade measured in years, not months.

The approach

We measured first. A two-week trend of CHW tons, tower approach, and after-hours loads. The building’s actual peak was 1,640 tons, not the 2,400 the original drawings assumed. We right-sized to 2 × 1,200 with N+1 at part load, and added a 400-ton heat-recovery chiller to pick up domestic hot water and morning warmup from heat that used to go to the sky.

Towers were replaced on a weekend crane window in March. Low-noise fans, VFDs, a new screen wall coordinated with the architect. Sequences rewritten: chilled-water reset, tower-approach control, and a lockout that keeps the heat-recovery machine first in the stack whenever the building is asking for heat.

DOB filings under the alteration type appropriate to equipment replacement. FDNY notification for the diesel fire pump adjacency during the crane day. PE-stamped plant drawings, energy model for the LL97 pathway, and a commissioning script that ran for three weeks after beneficial use.

The outcome

Plant electrical use down 28% year-over-year, weather-normalized. EUI 58 → 47. Property-line sound 4 dBA quieter at night. The heat-recovery machine now covers roughly 60% of annual DHW. The 2030 LL97 pathway holds without purchasing offsets. The operator inherited a sequence they can explain, and a trend dashboard they actually open.
