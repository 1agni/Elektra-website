07 · Upper East Side · Residences · 2024

Beekman penthouse

Location

Beekman Place adjacency, Upper East Side

Year

2024

Client

Private household / architect

Scope

Full-floor HVAC replacement in an occupied building: concealed VRF, dedicated outdoor air, wine room, millwork integration.

Systems

Heat-recovery VRF with indoor units in millwork returns, not soffits invented after the fact. A small DOAS with ERV for a tight envelope and a kitchen hood that actually extracts. Wine room as its own climate: 55°F, humidity held, compressor isolated from the dining wall. Outdoor units on existing dunnage, re-screened, with vibration isolation the terrace neighbor could not hear at NC-25 inside.

The problem

The apartment had been renovated around a split system that announced itself in every room. Returns in the floor had been covered by a later rug. The wine closet was a residential fridge in a millwork niche that could not hold summer. The board had just rewritten terrace rules after a neighbor complaint. The household would live in the back half during the work; the floors below would remain occupied. Quiet hours were not a suggestion.

The approach

We sat with the architect before the millwork shop drawings froze. Loads from the actual glass and the actual occupancy — guests, a staff room, a gym that would exist. Heat-recovery VRF because the gym and the glass corner would ask for opposite things on the same afternoon. Branch boxes in a closet the household already disliked; we made it a proper mechanical room with access the technician can use without moving art.

Freight only. Floor protection as a specification. A temporary cassette in the occupied bedrooms so August did not become a hotel. Outdoor work in two weekday mornings the terrace rules allowed, not a weekend improvisation. Refrigerant piping pressure-tested and documented; the board’s engineer received the packet without a chase.

The outcome

Bedrooms at a measured NC-25 with the indoor units on. Wine room stable through a humid August. The terrace neighbor’s complaint file stayed closed. The house manager has a one-page setpoint sheet and a name. We remain on a residential agreement: the same two technicians, seasonal, the desk if a zone drops.
