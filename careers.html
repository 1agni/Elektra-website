Careers

Eighty-five, plus you.

We hire for craft and judgment. Hudson Square and LIC. Paid internships. No theatrical language. Six open roles.

How we hire

Send a letter and a CV to hello@elektra.nyc with the role in the subject. A conversation. References we call. A walk through a job if it helps either of us. We decide slowly enough to be sure and quickly enough to be respectful. Relocation to New York is assumed unless the role says otherwise. We sponsor when the work and the person match; we will not use that as a lure.

The studio is calm. The field is not. You need to be able to do both, or to be excellent at one and honest about the other. Healthcare experience is treasured. So is a decade in a union shop. So is a PE who has actually stood on a roof. So is a refrigeration mechanic who can talk to a chef, and a residential tech who can talk to a super without treating the apartment like a plant.

Project Engineer

Hudson Square · Full-time · PE preferred

Loads, BOD, drawing sets, examiner comments, coordination with the superintendent. You will own two or three jobs at a time, not twelve. Healthcare or laboratory experience is an advantage. PE stamp or on the path. You will sit with Maya on the first ones. Requirements: mechanical engineering degree or equivalent experience, Revit literacy, a portfolio of sets you actually authored, comfort in a plant. NYC commercial, not light residential. Salary band discussed in the first conversation; we do not hide it in the third.

BAS Technician

LIC / citywide · Full-time

Sequences in the controller, graphics that look like the building, trends that a chief engineer will open. Niagara is common here; native is fine if you can make it behave. You will commission, not only install. Night work exists. Cyber hygiene is part of the job — we do not put plants on the open internet. Requirements: five years in commercial BAS, a record of sequences you can explain, a driver’s license, the ability to write a closeout. Union sites as assigned.

Site Superintendent

Field · Full-time

The roof, the dock, the hoist, the crane window, the night. You run occupied construction without making the floor below a story. Coordination with GCs who have their own weather. Luis is your principal. Requirements: a decade of NYC commercial mechanical, union literacy, a calm radio voice, a record of shutdowns that started on time. Healthcare a plus. You will not be asked to estimate in the van.

Estimator

Hudson Square · Full-time

Takeoffs that match the set, qualifications that match the risk, a number David Kim will stand next to in a room. Crane, after-hours, temporary tons, and the things a spreadsheet forgets. Requirements: commercial mechanical estimating in New York, familiarity with union labor rates, the ability to walk a plant and see the missing valve. We do not win by omitting. We win by being the studio that already knew.

Refrigeration technician

LIC / citywide · Full-time

Walk-ins, racks, ice, leak search, recovery that is legal, a kitchen that is still plating. After close is the default; dinner rush is the exception you are hired for. Rafael Mendoza’s bench. Requirements: EPA 608, a record of commercial refrigeration in New York food service or grocery, the manners to work around a chef, a driver’s license, comfort on a roof. Hood and makeup literacy is an advantage. Union sites as assigned.

Residential service technician

Citywide · Full-time

Occupied luxury homes. VRF, hydronic, the closet behind millwork, the terrace condenser, the super, the dog. Floor protection is a specification. Freight, not the passenger cab. Elena Lowe’s bench. Requirements: serious residential or light-commercial VRF experience, the judgment to work in a finished apartment, a clean manner with building staff, a driver’s license. We will teach our notes. We will not teach care.
