Residences · 02

A technician who knows the apartment.

24/7 priority. Seasonal before the weather. The closet, the terrace condenser, the wine room setpoint — already in the notes.

Why an agreement in a home

Luxury HVAC fails in season, in silence, and over millwork. An agreement is how we arrive already knowing the panel, the filter size, the board’s freight hours, and the dog’s name if the house manager has mentioned it. It is not a commercial route that “also does penthouses.” It is a small book of apartments with returning people.

What it includes

Spring and autumn visits that happen before the first extreme day. Filters as specified — not a substitute that fits. Condensate, strainers, a look at the outdoor units the terrace actually has. A wine room or gym checked as its own climate. Priority at the emergency desk. Labor for diagnosis on the visit; repairs quoted or included depending on the shape we wrote.

We keep two residential shapes, analogous to commercial but smaller: care (scheduled + priority) and care plus labor (the hours when a zone drops). Full parts coverage is rare in a home because the equipment mix is specific; when we offer it, the asset list is short and walked. We will not copy a commercial full-coverage page onto a townhouse.

The people

Named technicians. A house manager or owner gets a direct path that still logs through the desk, so nights are covered when that technician is off. Building staff know who we are. We do not send a different face every filter change; that is how you scratch a floor.

Commercial agreements are a sibling page if the same owner holds a tower. One account team can hold both.
