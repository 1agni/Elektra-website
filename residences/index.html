Residences

Where the architecture and the equipment are both serious.

Penthouses, townhouses, brownstones, high-floor co-ops and condos. Occupied. Finished. Quiet. Not a suburban split-system, not a coupon, not a PTAC and a smile.

Who this is for

Owners, architects, and GCs working in apartments and houses where the millwork is a specification and the board has a packet. Family offices and building staff who already know that the wrong contractor is how you lose a floor. We are not a residential franchise. We are the same mechanical practice that runs plants, applied to rooms that happen to be homes.

What “luxury residential” means here

Complexity, not a price slogan. Multi-zone VRF or hydronic in a landmarked envelope. A dedicated outdoor-air path that does not dump on a terrace. A wine room, a gym, a staff room, each with its own physics. Historic fabric that cannot take a soffit invented on Tuesday. Board rules, LPC if the façade or the roof is in play, a co-op corridor that is not a loading dock. If the job is a single condenser on a suburban pad, we are the wrong studio. If the job is a full-floor apartment that must remain livable while we work, we are in range.

White-glove is a method

Floor protection before tools. Freight elevator, never the passenger cab unless the building has no freight and the super has signed the exception. Quiet hours observed, not negotiated at the door. The doorman and the superintendent are part of the crew plan. We leave the apartment as a residence, not a jobsite that forgot. After-hours in a home is a courtesy to the household and to the floors below — not a way to cheapen the labor.

The work

Repair and emergency in occupied homes. Service agreements with technicians who know the apartment. Gut-renovation HVAC with the architect and the GC. Systems: VRF/VRV, hydronic, DOAS, specialty rooms. Air: filtration, humidity, the indoor climate a conservationist would recognize. See the pages. The threshold is seriousness — of the architecture, and of the equipment — not a number we printed to sound exclusive.
