Residences · 05

Air as a specified material.

Filtration, humidity, ventilation. Homes that are lived in, with cooking, guests, and the occasional wildfire week.

What we actually do

Measure. Outdoor air that is real, not a bath fan. Filtration at a MERV the machine can take — higher is not always kinder to a VRF indoor unit. Humidity as a winter problem in tight towers and a summer problem in old masonry. Cooking and a fireplace are loads, not lifestyle. We will not sell a glowing box that bypasses the system we just balanced.

Humidity

Steam humidifiers, evaporative, or the decision to run a dedicated outdoor-air unit that can dehumidify in shoulder seasons. Wood floors, instruments, and wine rooms notice. So does a brownstone that never had a vapor conversation. We specify, we commission, we tell you what the setpoint is for, in English.

Filtration and events

Wildfire weeks and demolition next door are why a bypass or a higher-grade filter rack exists. Healthcare-grade is a different practice; a home does not need an OR. It needs a system that can accept a better filter for a month without freezing a coil.
