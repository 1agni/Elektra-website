Privacy

Elektra Mechanical. 200 Hudson Street, Suite 800, New York, NY 10013. Effective 1 January 2026.

This page describes how the studio handles information you give us through this website, by email, or in the course of a proposal or a service contract. It is written for a fictional practice; treat it as a serious template, not as counsel.

What we collect

If you send the proposal form, we receive the fields you complete: name, company, email, building address, borough, system type, timeline, and message. If you call, we receive the number you use and whatever you tell the desk. If you apply for work, we receive the letter and CV you send. If you are a client, we receive the operational data required to design, install, or maintain a plant — drawings, trends, access lists, equipment tags.

This static website does not use advertising cookies, analytics pixels, or chat widgets. A server log on whatever host you place it on may record IP addresses and user agents; that is the host’s doing, and we do not sell it.

How we use it

To answer you. To decide if we are the right studio. To staff and file a job. To dispatch emergency. To consider you for a role. We do not sell lists. We do not share proposal contents with a competing bidder. Subcontractors and engineers of record see what they need for the work they are under contract to perform, under the same expectation of care.

BAS and building data

Credentials remain the owner’s. Trends we hold under a maintenance contract are for that contract. We do not place a plant on the public internet. We do not use building data to train a public model.

Retention

Proposals we did not win are kept long enough to answer a follow-up, then reduced to a name and a date unless you ask us to delete sooner. Contract records follow the law and the insurance carrier — typically seven years after closeout. Employment applications: one year unless hired. Emergency closeouts stay with the asset file if you become a client.

Your requests

Write to hello@elektra.nyc. Ask for a copy, a correction, or a deletion of what you sent through this site. We will say if a legal or contractual duty requires us to keep a piece of it. New York residents may have additional rights under state law; we will not use that sentence to avoid a reasonable request.

Children

The site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this page changes in a material way, we will date it. Continued use of the site after that date is the new text.
